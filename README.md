## About

It is 17:53 in Kiyoshima.

At 18:00, the island will be bombed.

You are a postman on Route Six with one final collection left to finish. You cannot stop what is coming. The only thing you can really change is how much of the route gets completed before it happens.

Seven Minute Shadow is a short collection of four increasingly stressful microgames tied together by one postal route.

The game takes some inspiration from WarioWare, mostly in the idea of throwing the player into simple challenges and asking them to understand them quickly, but I wanted to use that structure for something a bit different.

---

## The Game

- 4 different microgames
- 4 lives
- One continuous postal route
- Difficulty increases as the route goes on
- Music gradually gets more distorted as you get closer to 18:00
- A small archive ending based on how much mail was recovered (number of minigames you completed)
- Built in Godot
